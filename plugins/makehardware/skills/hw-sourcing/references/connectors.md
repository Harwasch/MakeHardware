## Principles that hold regardless of brand

* **Keyed and polarised** wherever a reversed insertion could damage anything.
* **Locking** for anything that moves, ships assembled, or carries power.
* **Pitch follows current and assembly method**, not board space. Do not put a
  1.0 mm pitch part where a technician has to hand-assemble it in the field.
* **One family per function per product.** Mixing three board-to-wire families
  on one board is an assembly error waiting to happen.
* **Check the mating half is stocked too.** A connector is only as available as
  its least available half, and the crimps and the tool count.
